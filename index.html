How To Read This Page
  • Source. The LNC's own FEC filings (committee C00255695): its reported totals for every year, and its itemized gifts from all 48 reports covering 2021 to 2024. The itemized gifts add up to the LNC's reported total on every report, to the cent.
  • Over $200 means over $200 in a calendar year. The FEC itemizes a donor once their gifts for the year pass $200, so a monthly donor at $25 counts as under $200 until late in the year.
  • July 2023 is under-reported. The LNC's July 2023 report itemizes only a handful of gifts; most regular donors that month were likely reported as unitemized. Yearly totals are unaffected, but July 2023 on its own should not be read as a real dip.
  • Checked independently. A separate review re-parsed all 48 filings and reproduced these figures; it found the missing March 2016 report and the size-band issue, both fixed here.
  • "Left" is not proof someone stopped giving. It means they did not give over $200 that year; they may have given less.
  • Matching. Donors are matched by name, then joined across ZIP codes so that someone who moved counts once. Two people with the same name who never gave in the same month could be joined by mistake; two entries of one person under different spellings stay apart.
  • No names. This page shows counts only. The donor-level table is kept in the project for research; federal law bars using FEC contributor names to ask for money.
  • The quotes were checked word for word against the LNC minutes, the meeting transcripts, and the memo as reprinted. The March 2023 speaker is not named in the recording. Naming a person at a step records their part in it, not fault.
